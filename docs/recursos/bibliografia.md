# Bibliografía oficial y recursos de consulta

Selección del programa académico, pp. 7–8, con recursos complementarios de la presentación del proyecto.

## Fundamentos

- Russell y Norvig. *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*, 4.ª ed. [Pearson y recursos del libro](https://aima.cs.berkeley.edu/).
- Google Cloud. [AI Hypercomputer](https://cloud.google.com/ai-hypercomputer) y [Cloud TPU](https://cloud.google.com/tpu/docs).
- NVIDIA. [CUDA C++ Programming Guide](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/) y [Deep Learning Performance Guide](https://docs.nvidia.com/deeplearning/performance/).
- Documentación de [TensorFlow](https://www.tensorflow.org/), [PyTorch](https://pytorch.org/docs/), [JAX](https://docs.jax.dev/), [PyTorch/XLA](https://docs.pytorch.org/xla/) y [OpenXLA](https://openxla.org/).

## Optimización y escalabilidad

- [JAX para cargas de IA](https://docs.jax.dev/), [benchmarking en JAX](https://docs.jax.dev/en/latest/benchmarking.html) y [OpenXLA](https://openxla.org/).
- NVIDIA. [TensorRT](https://docs.nvidia.com/deeplearning/tensorrt/). Google Cloud. [Cloud TPU Performance Guide](https://cloud.google.com/tpu/docs/performance-guide).
- Dean y Ghemawat. [MapReduce: Simplified Data Processing on Large Clusters](https://research.google/pubs/pub62/).
- Google Cloud. [Architecture Center](https://cloud.google.com/architecture), [guías de arquitectura IA/ML](https://cloud.google.com/architecture/ai-ml) y [Vertex AI](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/docs).
- Herramientas. [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/), [GitHub](https://github.com/), [Python](https://www.python.org/) y [Docker](https://docs.docker.com/).

## Lecturas complementarias

- Jouppi et al. (2017). [In-Datacenter Performance Analysis of a Tensor Processing Unit](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.04760).
- Jouppi et al. (2021). *Ten Lessons From Three Generations Shaped Google's TPUv4i*. El programa proporciona el portal [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/).
- Publicaciones de [Google Research](https://research.google/pubs/), [NVIDIA Research](https://research.nvidia.com/) y [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/research/).
